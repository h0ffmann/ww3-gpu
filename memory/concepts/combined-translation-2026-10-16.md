# The propagation and W3WAVE support units on Kokkos: twelve routines translated in one day, what is open

A lead, not evidence: every fact below has its record in the repository, named in brackets.

## What was done

On 2026-10-16, under the owner's standing order of that day (translate, easiest first, no test
run, bit-for-bit non-negotiable), two parallel sessions translated twelve WW3 routines to
Kokkos as header-only device functions [PR #149, the two plan docs
`docs/PROPAGATION_KOKKOS_PLAN_202610.md` and `docs/W3WAVE_SUPPORT_KOKKOS_PLAN_202610.md`,
issue #42]:

- The propagation package of `switch_lab_shrd` (`PR3`, `UQ`): `W3XYP3`, `W3KTP3`,
  `W3QCK1`, `W3QCK2`, `W3QCK3`, `WAVNU1` [`kokkos/src/ww_kokkos/prop_*.hpp`].
- The per-step support routines `W3WAVE` calls, plus `DISTAB` at set-up: `W3GATH`,
  `W3SCAT`, `W3UWND`, `W3DZXY`, `DISTAB`, `WAVNU2` [`wave_gath_scat.hpp`,
  `updt_uwnd.hpp`, `updt_dzxy.hpp`, `disp_distab.hpp`].

The method, as for ST4 and W3SRCE: the -O3 object of each Fortran module, built with WW3's
CMake Release flags inside the pinned ww3 shell (byte-identical to the CMake build's; outside
the shell the same gfortran wrapper gave another `w3dispmd.o`), read as GIMPLE and assembly;
one parser, one translator and one fresh reviewer per routine; nothing run, no test written
(ADR-0010, ADR-0011). The website's translated share moved to 99.3 % of the profiled run time
[`kokkos/PORT_STATUS.md`, regenerated by `scripts/port_score.py`].

## Lessons worth keeping

- A merge of the support branch as it stood would have shown `.kimi-code/RETRIEVAL.md` as
  deleted: the branch predated PR #142. Cherry-picking the two single commits onto a fresh
  branch from main avoided the skew [PR #149].
- The stopped work stayed marked: the W3UWND and DISTAB/WAVNU2 reviews never reported, the
  CUDA SASS of `disp_distab.hpp` holds 87 untraced fused multiply-adds (suspected from CUDA's
  tanh/cosh/sinh and division), and `W3UTRN` was never translated. Each is recorded as such in
  the port reports [`kokkos/port_report/`] rather than smoothed over.
- `W3XYP3` keeps the Fortran's out-of-bounds write below `VQ`, and its grid maps must be
  copied from the Fortran because `W3MAP3` reads `MAPSTA` out of bounds; both matter at
  wiring time [`prop_xyp3.hpp` heritage header].

## What comes next

1. Rerun the two stopped reviews: `W3UWND` and `DISTAB`/`WAVNU2`.
2. Trace the 87 FFMA in `disp_distab.hpp`'s CUDA SASS; compile the other three support

3. Translate `W3UTRN` (`model/src/w3updtmd.F90:2809-3194` at the upstream base).
4. Then the shims and the `W3WAVE`/`W3IOGR` hunks, then the ADR-0011 checkpoint:
