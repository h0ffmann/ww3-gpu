# Phase one copies per call

"Phase 1 copies per call and exists to validate; phase 2 keeps the spectrum resident"
(`AGENTS.md`) `(v)`. A phase-1 kernel pays the transfer on every call, so its CUDA column is not
"the model will be this much faster".

The phase exists to pass [[202610141900-bit-for-bit-is-the-gate]], and its numbers stay under
[[202610141903-no-timing-without-parity]].
