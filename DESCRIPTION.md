ww3-gpu: an open lab for the operational optimisation of WAVEWATCH III. It has a Nix-pinned toolchain, the DIA source-term kernel ported to C++/Kokkos with bit-exact parity against the Fortran on CPU and GPU, a per-field NetCDF comparator, and a 16-lesson course from the wave spectrum to bulk porting with agents. Groundwork for a UFRJ undergraduate project with LabECO/UFSC.
