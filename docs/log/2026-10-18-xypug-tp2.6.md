## Question

The resident preset stopped `ww3_tp2.6` (the `LIMON` unstructured grid, `EXPFSN = T`) at start-up,
because `XYP3` and `KTP3` did not cover `UNGTYPE`
([`2026-10-18-resident-regtest-lab-switch.md`](2026-10-18-resident-regtest-lab-switch.md)). With
`W3XYPUG` and `W3XYPFSN2` translated (port reports
[W3XYPUG](../../kokkos/port_report/W3XYPUG_KOKKOS.md),
[W3XYPFSN2](../../kokkos/port_report/W3XYPFSN2_KOKKOS.md)), do its two matrix entries give the
Fortran's bytes?

## What was done

On the owner's i9-14900 (32 hardware threads), ww3-gpu `d93be6d` (branch `goal/196-port-w3xypug`,
on #191's `daaef07`), `WW3/` at `84df86e0` (`ww3-gpu/xypug-196`), gfortran and g++ 16.2.0, Kokkos
5.2.2 OpenMP, started by the owner with `bash ~/code/ww3-gpu-3/test-branch.sh --bg` (a local
script) at 16:03 local time. It ran, in order:

1. `just kokkos-test serial-debug` and `openmp-release` (11/11 each), `bash scripts/ww3-gpu.sh
   --self-test`, `just ww3-gpu-matrix-selftest` (7 serial entries of the resident preset at
   `84df86e0`), `python3 scripts/port_score.py --check`.
2. `bash scripts/ww3-gpu.sh --ww3-gpu smoke --gpu --build-only --kokkos resident`: `ww_kokkos`
   `cuda-release` and WW3 built for CUDA, nothing run on the GPU. (A first step, `just
   kokkos-build cuda-release`, failed because that recipe runs in the CPU shell, whose gcc 16 nvcc
   13.0 refuses; it was the script's error and is removed.)
3. `WW_KOKKOS_VA_POISON=1 WW_KOKKOS_VA_COUNT=1 WW_KOKKOS_PROP3_COUNT=1 bash scripts/ww3-gpu.sh
   --ww3-gpu smoke --cpu --kokkos resident`.
4. `WW3GPU_KOKKOS=resident WW3GPU_LAB_SWITCH=ON just ww3-gpu-matrix 4 serial -R '^00[12]:'`.

The working tree matched `d93be6d` throughout; the commit was recorded two minutes after the start,
with no change to its files.

## What it changes

`W3XYPUG` and `W3XYPFSN2` are regtested on the one serial entry of the resident selection that runs
them, 2 of 2 rows. `kokkos/ports.tsv` records it in their `regtested` cells. A regtest with an
unstructured grid that turns on `FLCTH`/`FLCK` or `FLSOU` would cover `KTP3` and the source terms
there; none is in the resident selection at this pin.
