**The restart pair (gap 10), designed and not run.** `scripts/restart_pair.sh`, on the `full` case
(`kokkos/tests/fixtures/bench_small`) built with `switch_lab_shrd`:

```bash
WW3GPU_SWITCH=switch_lab_shrd bash scripts/ww3-gpu.sh --ww3-gpu full --cpu --kokkos resident --build-only
WW_KOKKOS_VA_POISON=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=4 nix develop picasso/labs/pratico#ww3 --command bash scripts/restart_pair.sh
```

It runs, with one executable, `fortran/a` and `kokkos/a` from 00:00 to 12:00 (nine fields every
hour from 06:00, a restart at 06:00 and 12:00) and `fortran/b` and `kokkos/b` from 06:00, each
from its own `a` run's 06:00 restart copied to `restart.ww3`. It compares with `cmp` `out_grd.ww3`
and the 12:00 restart of `b` against `a` in each mode, and every file of `kokkos/x` against
`fortran/x`; a Kokkos difference that the Fortran pair shows too is printed as WW3's own. Checked:
`bash -n`, `shellcheck` (clean) and `--self-test` (the namelist edits), nothing more ⚠.
